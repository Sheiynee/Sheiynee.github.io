03 / Engineering approach

Beyond the feature.

The important details are not just what changed, but why it needed to change and how the solution fits the existing system.

A / 01

Understand the workflow

Identify the operational requirement, the people involved, the expected state transitions, and the existing Odoo behavior before choosing a customization.

A / 02

Prefer native behavior

Work with existing models and business logic where practical. Add custom behavior where the requirement genuinely needs it, while respecting the constraints of the deployment environment.

A / 03

Validate edge cases

Consider permissions, company context, record visibility, unexpected states, and the effect of a change on related workflows.

Client-specific names, screenshots, data, and proprietary code are intentionally omitted. Detailed technical write-ups should only be published when they can be shared without violating confidentiality.
